# Provider logos: where each came from

Fetched 2026-10-13. Each mark is the provider's own artwork. Path data is
copied verbatim; the only changes are the wrapper (a 24-unit box, one
translate/scale to centre the mark) and, for marks published in black and
white, `currentColor` as the fill. They are trademarks of their owners, used
here only to identify the provider being connected.

| Provider | File | Source | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anthropic | `marks.ts` | Press kit from https://www.anthropic.com/press-kit, `Anthropic symbol - Slate.svg` | Published in Slate #141413 and Ivory #FAF9F5. Follows the text colour. |
| OpenAI | `marks.ts` | https://cdn.openai.com/brand/openai-logos.zip (linked from https://openai.com/brand), `OAI_OpenAI-Blossom_Black.svg` | Black or white only; the brand page says not to add colours to the Blossom. Follows the text colour. |
| Google Gemini | `gemini.svg` | https://www.gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_aurora_33f86dc0c0257da337c63.svg, as used by https://gemini.google.com | Google's colours, unchanged. The file carries its colour as an embedded image clipped to the sparkle, so it is loaded as an image, not inlined. |
| ElevenLabs | `marks.ts` | https://elevenlabs.io/brand, "ElevenLabs Symbol (SVG)" | Published in black and white. Follows the text colour. |
| Sarvam AI | `marks.ts` | https://assets.sarvam.ai/assets/brand/logos/sarvam-logo-black.svg, linked from https://www.sarvam.ai/brand | Published in black and white. Follows the text colour. Do not show below 20px. |
| Twilio | `marks.ts` | The symbol from https://www.twilio.com/content/dam/twilio-com/core-assets/customer-logos/t-z/twilio.svg (wordmark left out) | Twilio red #F22F46, unchanged. Twilio's brand portal needs a login, so its written guideline was not read. |
| Exotel | `marks.ts` | The "x" of the wordmark in the header of https://exotel.com, which Exotel uses alone as its favicon | #231F20 on light and white on dark on Exotel's site. Follows the text colour. Exotel publishes no standalone vector mark and no brand page was found. |
| Vobiz | `marks.ts` | https://www.vobiz.ai/favicon.svg, the mark Vobiz uses alone as its site icon (the same two shapes open its wordmark at https://www.vobiz.ai/whitelogo.svg) | Vobiz's two oranges, #E83C00 and #E86A00, unchanged. No brand guideline page was found. |

Not used: the `simple-icons` set. It has no OpenAI, Twilio, Sarvam, Exotel or Vobiz,
and its Gemini icon is the older, sharper sparkle.
